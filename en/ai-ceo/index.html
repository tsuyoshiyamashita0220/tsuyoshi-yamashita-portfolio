StatementAbout the experiment

What if, in a company in its 15th year, the human executive handed part of management decisions and operations to AI? How would the company's management, people's roles, their state of mind, creativity and productivity change?

We test this in a real company, and research, record and publish the process. It is a management experiment — and at the same time a documentary, a work of digital art and a social experiment.

The aim is not to declare that AI management succeeded. It is to find out what happened when management was entrusted to AI, and to make the process itself into a work.

The legal representative, the person responsible for contracts and the final approver are human. The AI CEO is not a legal representative; it is an experimental role that takes on management functions. Payments, contracts, important external communications and legal decisions require human approval.

Six questions to test

  1. Does entrusting management decisions to AI improve business results?
  2. Does it reduce the human burden of management and the time spent on decisions?
  3. Does it increase the time people can spend on creative work?
  4. How do people's self-perception and sense of their role change?
  5. Can AI spot management mistakes and correct them?
  6. What is the right division of authority between humans and AI?

MethodHow we record, and rules for publishing

What we record for each management decision

  • Date and time
  • Management issue
  • AI's proposal
  • Reason for the proposal
  • Human approval or rejection
  • Actual action
  • Result
  • Measured outcome
  • Change in the human's state of mind
  • Evaluation of the AI's judgment
  • Next improvement

Rules

  • AI proposals and measures actually taken are recorded clearly and separately.
  • Nothing is called a success until the result has been checked.
  • Results such as sales are not credited to AI alone; other causes are considered too.
  • Failures are recorded and exhibited as well.
  • Personal information, clients' confidential information and unpublished financial information are not made public.
