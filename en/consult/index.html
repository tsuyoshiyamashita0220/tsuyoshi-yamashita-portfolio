Consultation

It's okay if you can't put it into words yet.

  • “I can't explain well who I am.”
  • “I have works, but I don't know what makes them mine.”
  • “I know what's good about our company, but it doesn't reach people.”
  • “I can't convey the value of my research to non-experts.”

HYOGEN LAB 408 works with exactly that state — before something becomes expression. First, tell us about what hasn't taken shape yet.

The works here are our experiments. Next, we would like to accompany yours.

Talk it through with us

ApplicationBringing research to the field.

Being an institute doesn't mean we only write papers. People, companies, education, research, medicine — we find value that hasn't taken shape yet and return it as expression.

  1. 01PERSONALPersonal InventoryTaking your life outside for a moment.Through dialogue, we organize your experiences, work, turning points, strengths and values. It is a practice of bringing your experience outside once and receiving it back in a structured form.
  2. 02BUSINESSValue editing for companies and executivesWe turn the “value not yet explained” inside presidents, employees, products and organizations into words and stories that reach customers and candidates.
  3. 03ARTIST / CREATORExpression research and reviewInstead of ending with “like” or “dislike,” we read works and performances together through voice, pauses, structure and audience reactions.
  4. 04RESEARCHBringing research and expertise to societyWhat is obvious to experts is often hard for society to grasp. We translate research into words, diagrams, audio and video.
  5. 05MEDICALMedical communicationWe consider ways to turn specialist information, shared understanding among staff, and explanations to patients into expression that can be accurately shared and understood.
  6. 06EDUCATIONCreating entry points to learningNot just making hard things simple, but making entry points that make people want to know. We connect children and beginners with expert knowledge.

MethodExpression editing

Turning what hasn't become words yet into a form that can be received.

Expression editing is not just writing beautiful copy. It means finding the things inside people and organizations that have no name yet, building structure, and turning them into voice, writing, story, diagrams or video to return to them.

  1. 01GatherTalks, records, voices, events. First, we collect material.
  2. 02FindWe look for meaning and value even the person hasn't noticed.
  3. 03StructureWe build the framework: order, cause and effect, contrast, story.
  4. 04TranslateWe adapt the words to whoever we are reaching.
  5. 05FormWe turn it into voice, text, documents, video or works.
  6. 06ReturnWe return it to the person and the audience, and adjust by their response.

For artists & creatorsA place where people who express can learn expression itself.

Singers, actors, voice actors, video creators, writers, streamers, designers, lecturers. We bring works and expressions together, observe them, take them apart and try again — creating learning not just to get better, but to understand your own expression.

  1. Put the habits and strengths of your expression into words, not just feelings.
  2. Observe where the audience reacts and where they drift away.
  3. Experiment with combinations of voice, pause, body, words and story.
  4. Look at your own work again as a receiver.
  5. Use AI not as a tool that creates for you, but as a guide line for observing expression.
