ExperiencesExperiences

These works respond to your voice, sound and hands. Works that use the microphone ask for permission only when you press the start button inside the work. The work itself is displayed in Japanese.

  • Interactive

    VOICE BLOOM

    An interactive work in which a different flower grows from the sound of each voice.

    You need: A microphone (without one, you can use a recording or the demo sound)

    Experience VOICE BLOOM
  • Interactive

    MUSIC DNA FLOWER

    Let it listen to a song, and a flower is born from the song's structure.

    You need: An audio file, or a microphone

    Experience MUSIC DNA FLOWER
  • Interactive

    This Testimony Is a Lie.

    A single-player mystery game: find contradictions in testimonies to get closer to the truth. Set in the Kitakyushu Science and Research Park, Hibikino.

    You need: Nothing in particular

    Start the game
