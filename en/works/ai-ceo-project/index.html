No.12　AIは社長になれるのか？ ― 人間とAIの判断の境界を探る実験

Can AI Be a CEO? — An Experiment on the Boundary Between Human and AI Judgment

Research & ExperimentsManagement experiment · DocumentaryOngoing#AI and people#Management#Ways of working

A research experiment that entrusts part of our own company's management decisions to AI, recording AI proposals, human decisions and actual results separately.

Go to the experiment exhibit

RecordDetails

If a human executive entrusted part of management decisions and operations to AI, how would the company and its people change? We test this in a real company and research, record and publish the process.

The legal representative, the person responsible for contracts and the final approver are human. The AI CEO is an experimental role.

In the gallery, progress will be published in six rooms as the special exhibition “AI CEO — 365 DAYS.”
