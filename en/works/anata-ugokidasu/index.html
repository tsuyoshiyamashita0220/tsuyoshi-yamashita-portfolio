Take part

Touch, speak, stay silent. An experimental work whose screen responds to what the viewer does.

You need　A microphoneMicrophone permission is requested when you press “Take part” inside the work.The work itself is displayed in Japanese.

About the workAbout the work

Expression is completed by response.Words that accompany the work

Made as the institute's “Experiment 001.” It is not a video to just watch: when you touch, speak or stay silent, the screen does something too.

It uses the microphone. Sound is not sent outside this device.
