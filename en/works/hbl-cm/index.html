HYOGEN BUSINESS LEARNING 60-second commercial

A 60-second video introducing our work making operation manuals and training materials, using a fictional manufacturing company as an example.

You need　Nothing in particularAn on-screen animation (no audio file is included). You can play, pause and move between scenes.The work itself is displayed in Japanese.
Watch the video

About the workAbout the work

It shows how work procedures that exist only in the heads of staff on site are turned, through interviews, into materials anyone can learn from.

The video goes through “issue,” “request,” “interviews” and “production and review.” Content related to safety and work procedures is finally checked by the client's site manager.

The company, conversations and manuals in the video are all a fictional demo.
