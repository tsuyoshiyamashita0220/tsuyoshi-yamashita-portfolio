Experience MUSIC DNA FLOWER

Let it listen to a song, and a flower is born from the song's structure.

You need　An audio file, or a microphoneYou can use an audio file you have.The work itself is displayed in Japanese.
Experience MUSIC DNA FLOWER

About the workAbout the work

The shape of the song becomes a flower.Words that accompany the work

Play music into the microphone or choose an audio file, and it reads the song's flow over time — loudness, brightness, amount of change and section breaks — and turns it into a flower.

Touch a petal to see which part of the song it came from. The same audio and the same seed number always produce the same flower.

“DNA” in the title refers to the data recording how the song changes over time. It does not analyze genes.

Audio is analyzed only on this device and is not sent anywhere.
