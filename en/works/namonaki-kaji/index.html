Nameless Chores Check — Solo Edition

Education & DialogueWeb checkComplete#Home#Looking at people
Start the solo check

A check you answer alone: home tasks broken into 312 items, showing how much you carry and your type of sharing.

You need　One person (5–10 minutes; you can save and continue later)Answers are handled only on this device.The work itself is displayed in Japanese.
Start the solo check

About the workAbout the work

Giving names to the work that has no name.Words that accompany the work

You can count what you did, but not what you thought about before doing it — planning meals, remembering deadlines, sensing someone's mood. This check counts home tasks in 312 items, including that nameless work.

You choose your household type, estimate your share by gut feeling before checking, then answer each item as “me, half, partner, or outside help.” The results also show the gap between your estimate and the actual numbers.

Time spent with your hands and the “mental load” of deciding, remembering and paying attention are measured separately and compared on a 100-cell grid. Even in the same home, the two rarely match.

The 312 items fall into four types: information (remembering, looking up), judgment (deciding), physical (moving hands and feet) and emotional (sensing, soothing). Ways of sharing divide into 16 types along four axes — not personality, but the current imbalance in how tasks are carried.

The results list “what to let go of first” (heavy items that are easy to hand over), estimates of what you'd save, and a “memo for talking it over” you can show your partner as is.

Time estimates assume a typical household and will differ from yours. No name or email is needed, and neither your answers nor results leave your device. This is not a medical or psychological diagnosis.
