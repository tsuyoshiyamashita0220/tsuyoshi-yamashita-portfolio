About the workAbout the work

Humans are a hassle. That's what makes them interesting.Words that accompany the work

Each word comes with its ordinary dictionary meaning and its “human-speak” meaning. For example, “busy”: in the dictionary, “having many things to do and no free time.” In human-speak, “somehow there's always time left to say you're busy.”

Volume 1 contains 100 words. You read it by turning pages like a book, with a table of contents, search and bookmarks. (The work is in Japanese.)
