Voice-acting classes at a public school (featured on NHK)

Education & DialogueClassDone

A class on expression using the voice (a voice-acting experience) at a public elementary school, featured on NHK news as “a job experience popular with children.”

RecordDetails

We taught children at a public elementary school how to express with their voices (a voice-acting experience).

Holding scripts and standing in front of microphones, they played roles with their voices. The class was featured on NHK news as “a job experience popular with children.”
