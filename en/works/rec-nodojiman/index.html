THE Nodojiman (karaoke contest)

Practice in Business & SocietyEventDone

One of the largest karaoke contests in western Japan, held over four years.

RecordDetails

One of the largest karaoke contests in western Japan, held for four years from 2016 to 2019.

The summer 2019 contest, “THE Nodojiman Chō!!!”, was held on Saturday, August 24 at Kurosaki Hibishin Hall. It was open to ages 3 to 118, with 60 participants recruited.

It was a tournament from the first round to the final, with prize money for each round won. Winners were decided by votes from performers and the audience. Prizes for the winner included a guest appearance on FM kitaQ's “Suiyō Golden,” prize money and a certificate, and a professional recording experience.
