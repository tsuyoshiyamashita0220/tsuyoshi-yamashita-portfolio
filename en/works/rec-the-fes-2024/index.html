Festival of Performers: THE FES 2024

Practice in Business & SocietyEventDone

A festival of performers held on January 8, 2024 (Coming of Age Day) at the Small Theater of J:COM Kitakyushu Performing Arts Center. Certified as a Kitakyushu City 60th anniversary project. More than 20 acts.

RecordDetails

A festival where performers of different genres — classical, singing, voice acting, idols — share one stage in a single day. It was certified as a Kitakyushu City 60th anniversary project.

From the 1:30 pm opening to 8:30 pm, it ran in five parts: Part 1 Classical Time, Part 2 Artist Time, Part 3 Actor Time, Part 4 Idol Time, Part 5 Artist Time.

The opening act was 小倉爆笑劇場. DJ: City Loris. MCs: 祥泰 and 坂本明日香. Tsuyoshi Yamashita also performed in Part 5.
