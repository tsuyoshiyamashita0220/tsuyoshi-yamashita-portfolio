About the workAbout the work

On the day you were born, which mansion was the moon in?Words that accompany the work

The Sukuyō-kyō is an astrological scripture that Kūkai brought back to Japan more than 1,200 years ago. From the moon's position on your birthday, it finds your “honmei-shuku” among 27 lunar mansions.

The old lunisolar calendar is reconstructed by astronomical calculation and checked against all twelve month names recorded in the Sukuyō-kyō (according to the work's description).

Enter your date of birth and a disc turns, stopping with your mansion at the top of the ring. You can also read about the Four Symbols — guardian beasts dividing the 28 mansions into east, west, south and north — compatibility between two mansions using the “three-nine method,” and comparisons of original texts, calendar methods and sources.

Items carry color marks: green for confirmed facts, gold for calculation rules, and yellow for items not yet calculated because schools differ.

Your date of birth is not saved or sent anywhere. Supported years are 1901–2099. Sukuyō interpretations are a traditional way of thinking, not scientific fact (as stated in the work).
