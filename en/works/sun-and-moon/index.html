About the workAbout the work

The me I can see, and the me I can't see yet.Words that accompany the work

There are 12 sun cards and 12 moon cards. Choose one of each and the two overlap, opening a short piece written for that pair (with a small suggestion for today, a phrase, and a question to yourself). There are 144 combinations in all.

This gallery shows the trial version, where you can choose from 2 sun cards and 2 moon cards — 4 combinations.
