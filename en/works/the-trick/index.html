THE TRICK: Encyclopedia of World Mystery Tricks

Research & ExperimentsDictionaryPrototype#Mystery#Words
Open the dictionary

A prototype dictionary that classifies tricks from mystery fiction around the world by principle.

You need　Nothing in particularPrototype. Entries describe how tricks work, so be careful if you haven't read the works.The work itself is displayed in Japanese.
Open the dictionary

About the workAbout the work

It collects tricks from mystery works and organizes them by principle. You can choose to display it in Japanese, English or Chinese.

The categories and names of tricks are still provisional. Each entry shows whether it was checked against the original text or against commentaries and encyclopedias.
