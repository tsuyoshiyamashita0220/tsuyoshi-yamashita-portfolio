VOICE BLOOM

Experience VOICE BLOOM

An interactive work in which a different flower grows from the sound of each voice.

You need　A microphone (without one, you can use a recording or the demo sound)The work does not play sound. Microphone permission is requested only when you press “Start with microphone.”The work itself is displayed in Japanese.
Experience VOICE BLOOM

About the workAbout the work

There is no right voice. Only different ways of blooming.Words that accompany the work

When you use your voice, a bud first listens. From roughly the first 2.5 seconds, it reads sound features such as pitch, resonance and breathiness, which decide the flower's shape, color and texture. The more you keep using your voice, the more the flower opens.

Only the physical features of the sound are used. It does not estimate personality, emotion, health or skill, and no flower is better or worse than another.

The flower can also respond to the meaning of words: it shines at “thank you,” is hurt by harsh words, and grows a new bud at “I'm sorry.” Spoken-word input uses the browser's speech recognition, which sends audio outside the device, so it is off by default in this gallery. You can type words in the input box instead and try it without sending anything.

Besides the microphone, you can also make flowers bloom with a recording or the demo sound (synthesized). You can save the flower as an image.
