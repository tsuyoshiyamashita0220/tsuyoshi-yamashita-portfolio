Nameless Chores Check — Duo Edition

Start the check for two

Two people answer the same 60 questions separately, and only the points where their views of the housework split are listed. Subtitle: “Are you doing it? Can you see it?”

You need　Two people (one shared device or two devices)Takes 3–5 minutes. Answers are handled only on this device.The work itself is displayed in Japanese.
Start the check for two

About the workAbout the work

Counting what can't be seen.Words that accompany the work

When things get awkward over housework, maybe the problem isn't that someone “isn't doing it” but that it “isn't seen.” This check for two was born from that idea.

Both answer the same 60 questions as “me, half, partner, outside help, or none.” Neither can see the other's answers until both have finished. You can pass one device back and forth, or send it to the other's device by QR code or link.

It measures not the amount of housework, but only where your answers differ. It does not score each task. It is built to let you check, in your own home, a well-known tendency in psychology: when two people are asked what share of a joint effort was theirs, the total goes over 100%.

The results list by name the share over 100% when your claims are added (overlap), the share under 100% (each thinks the other does it), “you as seen by your partner,” and items you both answered “me” or both answered “partner.”

The 60 questions were chosen from the solo edition's 312 items so that all 6 areas and 40 subcategories are included.

No name or email is needed; answers are handled only on the device and sent nowhere. This is not a medical or psychological diagnosis.
